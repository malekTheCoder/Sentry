# Sentry

Sentry is a system monitor for your Mac's menu bar — CPU, memory, disk, temperatures, fans, battery, and network, with companion apps for iPhone and Apple Watch.

**[Download Sentry for Mac (.dmg)](https://github.com/malekTheCoder/Sentry/releases/latest/download/Sentry.dmg)**

Requires macOS 14 or later.
